The app works, but it talks like an engineer

30 September 2026 · Based on hands-on testing notes and a walkthrough of the running app

Every step of the pipeline does its job. What holds the app back is how it presents that work: it switches between Dutch and English, asks non-technical users to choose AI models by their technical names, and rarely says what it just finished. A handful of focused changes would make it usable by the people it is built for.

The three things that matter most

  1. No mixed languages, plain words.One screen shows “Vorige run beschikbaar”, “Drop your CSV file here” and “Check uitvoeren” side by side. Terms like Vector DB, reranker, GGUF and not_run mean nothing to our users.
  2. Always show what happened.After a step finishes, the app only offers to run it again. Model downloads show no sign of progress. After a restart, nothing says your earlier work is still there, even though it is.
  3. Hide the machinery.The Insights step offers 4 generate buttons, 5 filter combinations, a slider and 3 model choices. Most users need one button with good defaults, and an “Advanced” section for the rest.

How we reviewed it

We combined three sources. First, notes taken while using the app as a first-time user. Second, a walkthrough of every screen in the running app, captured in the screenshots below. Third, two design checklists: the UI/UX Pro Max guidelines (progress feedback, empty states, consistency) and the Impeccable design review, which also scanned the code and flagged 18 visual style issues.

Grades run from 1 (blocks the user) to 5 (excellent). A typical first working version scores between 2 and 3.

What we found, screen by screen

Across the whole pipeline

  • Step names describe the technology, not the job. We suggest: Anonymize Data → Remove personal details; Build Vector DB → Prepare answers for search; Rerank Themes → Double-check themes; Generate Insights → Write summaries; AI Query → Ask a question.
  • No sense of where you are. The tabs look the same whether a step is done or not. Replace them with a stepper that marks finished steps and points to the next one.

Suggested stepper: finished steps stay green after a restart, and the current step is highlighted.

Choosing an AI model (Build search index and Insights steps)

Users are asked to pick between “Octen Embedding 0.6B / 4B / 8B” and “Gemma 4 E2B / E4B / 12B QAT”. The descriptions mention “dynamic Q4”, “MTP” and “Hugging Face”. A stakeholder can’t make this choice, so we should make it for them and explain the trade-off in everyday terms.

QuickGood for a first look. Smallest download (2.6 GB).
Balanced (recommended)Best mix of speed and quality for most reports.
Most thoroughBest quality for the final report. Slowest.
Technical model names move to an “Advanced” section.

When a model is not on the computer yet, the app downloads it without saying so. Show a clear message such as “Downloading the Balanced model, 1.2 of 5 GB”, with a progress bar and an estimate.

Remove personal details (Anonymise)

  • Works well: results are split by type (names, locations, personal numbers, health), and the one possible miss is easy to spot.
  • Mixed light and dark: the progress panel is dark while the rest is light. Pick one light style for the whole app.
  • The three sub-steps look like separate apps. Group them visually as parts of one step (Upload → Choose columns → Check results), with one shared frame.
  • After finishing: replace the 0% panel with a short summary, such as “Done. 300 answers checked, 409 personal details removed, 1 to review.”

Prepare answers for search (Vector DB)

  • Keep the four-stage progress bar and reuse it in every step.
  • Move log lines such as “Loading Octen/Octen-Embedding-0.6B via SentenceTransformer on Apple GPU (mps)” behind a “Show details” link.
  • Same light/dark clash as the Anonymise step.

Double-check themes (Theme reranker)

A non-technical user has no idea what a reranker is. The screen shows a raw model ID (zeroentropy/zerank-2-reranker), a status of not_run and a model called “disabled”.

Suggested explanation on screen: “Some answers could fit more than one theme. This step lets a second, more careful AI read those answers again and pick the best theme. Answers that were already clear are left alone.”

We also suggest showing how many answers are affected, and translating statuses into words: “Not done yet”, “Done”, “Switched off”.

Write summaries (Insights)

  • Too many choices. Replace the four buttons with one: “Write summaries”. Offer one extra option: “Also write summaries per year, location and programme (takes longer)”.
  • Hard to get back. After running one option, the others are hard to find again. Show all results in one list with their status (done, in progress, not started).
  • Move to Advanced: the number of documents per theme, clearing the cache, and starting the language model server.

Overview dashboard after a restart

  • Never show the word “error” as data. If summaries haven’t been written yet, say so and link to the step that creates them.
  • Show when the data was last updated, so users know their work was kept.
